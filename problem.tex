\section*{Model}
For $N\geq2$, take Pauli operators $X_i,Y_i,Z_i$ and
\begin{equation}
H=\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}\left[J(X_iX_{i+1}+Y_iY_{i+1})+
\Delta Z_iZ_{i+1}\right].
\end{equation}
The Lindblad equation is $\dot\rho=-i[H,\rho]+\sum_a
(L_a\rho L_a^\dagger-\{L_a^\dagger L_a,\rho\}/2)$, with
\begin{align}
L_{L,+}&=\sqrt{\gamma(1+\mu)}\,\sigma_1^+,&
L_{L,-}&=\sqrt{\gamma(1-\mu)}\,\sigma_1^-,\\
L_{R,+}&=\sqrt{\gamma(1-\mu)}\,\sigma_N^+,&
L_{R,-}&=\sqrt{\gamma(1+\mu)}\,\sigma_N^-.
\end{align}
Here $\gamma\geq0$ and $|\mu|\leq1$. The package starts at
$\rho(0)=I/2^N$.
\section*{Continuity}
The Hamiltonian gives $\dot Z_i=j_{i-1}-j_i$ in the bulk, where
\begin{equation}
j_i=2J(X_iY_{i+1}-Y_iX_{i+1}).
\end{equation}
The left and right dissipative source terms in $\dot{\langle Z\rangle}$
are respectively $2\gamma(\mu-\langle Z_1\rangle)$ and
$-2\gamma(\mu+\langle Z_N\rangle)$. At stationarity, every bond
current equals the left source and the negative right source.
For $J=\Delta=0$, the edge spins relax from zero magnetization to
$\pm\mu(1-e^{-2\gamma t})$ and every interior spin remains unpolarized.
\section*{Matrix product density operator}
The local Liouville basis is
$|\uparrow\rangle\langle\uparrow|,
|\downarrow\rangle\langle\uparrow|,
|\uparrow\rangle\langle\downarrow|,
|\downarrow\rangle\langle\downarrow|$.
Fusing each ket/bra pair yields a four-dimensional MPS site, equivalent
to an MPO physical pair. The package also exposes an explicit MPO with
separate physical legs. Each bond generator contains its Hamiltonian
commutator; the boundary dissipators are assigned to the first or last
bond. Exponentiating these $16\times16$ generators gives local gates.
A forward sweep of half-step gates followed by a reverse sweep gives
second-order Lie--Trotter--Suzuki evolution. SVD truncation is controlled
by cutoff and maximum bond dimension. Compression does not guarantee
positivity, so trace, Hermiticity, and small-system eigenvalues are checked.
The dense reference constructs a full $2^N\times2^N$ Hamiltonian and
Lindblad superoperator separately.
\section*{Context}
Boundary-driven XXZ steady states are studied by T. Prosen,
\emph{Phys. Rev. Lett.} 107, 137201 (2011),
\texttt{https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.2978}. The package tests finite-chain
numerics and does not attempt an exact large-$N$ result.
